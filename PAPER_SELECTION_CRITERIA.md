\# EvidenceLens Paper Selection Criteria



\## Pilot Investigation Claim



CD24 expression is higher in primary breast carcinoma tissue than in patient-matched normal breast tissue.



\## Include a Paper When



\- It studies human breast cancer.

\- It investigates ERBB2/HER2 expression or closely related molecular evidence.

\- It includes primary experimental or observational research.

\- It reports enough methodological information to interpret the findings.

\- Its abstract is available through PubMed.

\- Preferably, its full text is legally accessible through PubMed Central or

&#x20; another open-access source.

\- It can support, contradict, clarify, or provide relevant context for the claim.



\## Exclude a Paper When



\- It studies only another cancer type.

\- It is a review, editorial, commentary, protocol, or case report.

\- It focuses only on treatment response without relevant ERBB2 expression data.

\- It uses only animal models or cell lines.

\- It provides insufficient information to assess the claim.

\- It is a duplicate publication.



\## Evidence Classifications



\### Support



The study directly reports higher ERBB2 expression in breast-tumor tissue

than in the relevant normal-tissue comparison.



\### Contradict



The study directly reports no increase or lower ERBB2 expression in tumor

tissue compared with the relevant normal-tissue comparison.



\### Unclear



The study investigates relevant samples or measurements, but its findings

cannot confidently establish whether the claim is supported or contradicted.



\### Not Applicable



The paper mentions ERBB2 or breast cancer but does not test the relationship

described in the claim.



\## Pilot Target



Select five papers first. Evaluate the pilot collection before expanding it

to approximately 15–20 papers.
