# EvidenceLens

## Project Description

EvidenceLens is an AI-powered biomedical claim investigation platform. It examines breast cancer claims across scientific papers, organizes supporting and conflicting evidence, provides traceable citations, and validates selected gene-expression claims using a public biological dataset.

## Target Users

Bioinformatics students and early-career biomedical researchers.

## Main Workflow

1. Enter a biomedical claim.
2. Find relevant studies.
3. Classify evidence as supporting, contradicting, unclear, or not applicable.
4. Inspect exact evidence passages and citations.
5. Compare study methods, samples, findings, and limitations.
6. Validate selected claims using gene-expression data.

## MVP Scope

- 15–20 breast cancer research papers
- One public gene-expression dataset
- Approximately five selected genes
- Evidence classification
- Exact supporting passages
- Structured study comparison matrix
- Statistical analysis and visualizations
- Basic system evaluation

## Not Included in the MVP

- Medical diagnosis or treatment recommendations
- User accounts
- Payments
- Large-scale paper ingestion
- Complex multi-agent workflows
- Training a custom language model

## Future Expansion

- Alzheimer’s disease research collection
- Additional biological datasets
- Automated PubMed paper collection
- Saved investigations and user accounts

## Disclaimer

EvidenceLens is designed for research and educational purposes. It does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment advice.
